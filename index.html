Flip finder

Finds items you can buy low and sell high on the Grand Exchange, with the 2% sale tax already taken off. Prices come live from the OSRS Wiki.

Buy at is the price instant sellers got, and sell at is the price instant buyers paid, so your offers need to sit around those prices to fill. Profit each is the sell price minus the buy price minus the 2% sale tax (rounded down, capped at 5,000,000, none under 50 gp, bonds exempt). Other tax-exempt items aren’t listed here, so their real profit is a little higher. “Traded” is the smaller of the buy-side and sell-side trade counts over the window, which shows how easily you can get in and out. Price age is how old the older of the two prices is. Old or one-off trades can make a flip look better than it is, so check the offers in game before you buy.
