Flip f‌inder

Finds items you can buy low and sell high on the Grand Exchange. Prices come live from the OSRS Wiki.

Buy at is the price instant sellers got, and sell at is the price instant buyers paid, so your offers need to sit around those prices to fill. Profit each is the sell price minus the buy price. “Traded” is the smaller of the buy-side and sell-side trade counts over the window, which shows how easily you can get in and out. Price age is how old the older of the two prices is. Old or one-off trades can make a flip look better than it is, so check the offers in game before you buy.

Each row is one make: the ingredients in the recipe column turn into the product. Cost uses the buying style you pick, and the sell value uses the selling style you pick. Makes in window is limited by the buy limits and trade counts of every ingredient and the product, and by your cash if you set a limit. Potions are made as 3-dose potions. This ignores burn and failure rates, minor extras like thread, and the value of the experience.

Average loot per casket

Reward caskets can’t be traded, so they have no price. These are the expected loot values the Old School Wiki works out from the drop tables, rounded. They use main-game prices from when the wiki last updated them, so they drift and don’t reflect your world. Click a heading to sort.

Items that drop from clues

Prices are from the world you picked above and show the middle of the buy and sell price. Only a selection of the better known clue items is listed. “Shared” items, such as god pages and blessings, can drop from any tier from easy up. Items with no recent trades in this world aren’t shown.

Type at least two letters to search.

Price, low and high come from the wiki prices for the world you picked above, and price is the middle of low and high. The offer columns are the open Grand Exchange offers on the Hardcore Worlds from the Hardcore World Tool Kit, which only knows about offers seen by players using its plugin, so an item can have offers it hasn’t seen. Offers are read from each item’s order book at the Tool Kit, so the best prices and counts should match the open offers shown on its own site. Only the closest 20 matches are looked up, one request each, and they’re remembered for a minute. Offers are always for the Hardcore Worlds, even if you switch the page to the main game.
